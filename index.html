Company
Overview

Moderna is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company that pioneered the use of messenger RNA (mRNA) as a new class of medicine. Founded in 2010 by Noubar Afeyan, Robert Langer, and Derrick Rossi, the company has redefined what a drug can be.

Unlike traditional drugs, Moderna's platform instructs the body's own cells to produce therapeutic proteins — a fundamentally new approach to medicine.

Challenges & Risks

Despite revolutionary success, Moderna faces significant headwinds that test the durability of its platform.

01
Revenue Cliff Post-COVID
COVID vaccine demand collapsed from $19.3B (2022) to ~$6.8B (2023). Heavy dependence on a single product creates financial volatility.
02
Cold Chain & Storage
mRNA vaccines require ultra-cold storage (−20°C), limiting deployment in low-resource settings and driving up logistics costs.
03
Patent Disputes
Ongoing IP battles with BioNTech/Pfizer and NIH over LNP delivery patents threaten royalty revenues and competitive moat.
04
Regulatory Uncertainty
mRNA therapies for non-vaccine applications face longer FDA review timelines. Novel modality = limited precedent for approval pathways.
05
Vaccine Hesitancy
Public skepticism about mRNA safety, amplified by misinformation, continues to slow adoption of future mRNA therapeutics.
